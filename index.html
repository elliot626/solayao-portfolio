About me

My coursework centers on computer networking and IT systems. I'm most comfortable when I can follow a problem step by step: what is connected, what is set up, and where it breaks.

I work well with other people. I try to explain technical ideas in plain words, ask questions early, and keep group tasks moving.

When I need code, I use AI tools to get a first version, which is often called “vibe coding.” Then I read it, run it, and fix it until it does what I need. This portfolio was made that way.
